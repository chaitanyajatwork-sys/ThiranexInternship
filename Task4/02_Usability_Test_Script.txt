AirSense AI — Usability Testing Script

Introduction:
“We are testing the AirSense AI interface, not you. Please think aloud while completing the tasks.”

Tasks:
1. Find the current AQI for your location.
2. Decide whether outdoor activity is recommended.
3. Find today's health advice.
4. Check the short-term forecast.
5. Set an alert for poor air quality.
6. Return to the Home screen.

After-task questions:
- What was easiest?
- What was confusing?
- Was the AQI easy to understand?
- Was the recommended action clear?
- What would you change?

Moderator notes:
Do not lead the participant. Record time, errors, hesitation, assistance, and comments.
